About us

Our story

How a few friends with a love for cars became a community of more than a hundred members in Astana.

Since 2022

From a parking lot to a real community

Apex Auto Club began with five friends who met every Saturday morning to talk about cars. Word spread, more people came, and soon we needed a plan, rules and a place to share everything online.

Today we organise meets, photo walks, workshops and track days. Our goal is simple: give car lovers in Astana a safe, friendly place to meet and learn.

Respect

Every car and every owner is welcome.

Safety

No street racing. Fast driving only on closed tracks.

Learning

We share skills through workshops and talks.

Clean spaces

We leave every location cleaner than we found it.
