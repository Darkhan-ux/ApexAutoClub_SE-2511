What we do

More than just parking lots

Every month we plan something new, so there is always a reason to take your car out.

Cars & Coffee meets

Relaxed morning meets where any car is welcome. Talk builds, swap tips and make new friends.

Photo walks

Golden-hour and night sessions in the city. Learn to shoot your car like a pro — even with a phone.

Workshops & drives

Detailing classes, basic maintenance and ice driving days with instructors from partner garages.

Who we are

Built by enthusiasts, open to everyone

We started as a few friends meeting on weekends. Today the club connects students, families and collectors who share one thing: a love for cars.

  • Any car, any budget — it's about people, not price tags.
  • Safe, respectful meets with clear rules.
  • Free membership and a personal profile on this site.
